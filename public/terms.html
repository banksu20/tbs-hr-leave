Terms of Service

Last updated: 8 October 2026

TBS HR Leave supports leave administration for TBS Marketing employees and company administrators. These terms describe appropriate use of the system. For support, contact ratchadale26@gmail.com.

Use of the system

Use your own employee or linked LINE account and provide accurate request details. Only authorized company staff may perform administrative actions. Do not access or change another person’s records without authorization, misuse approval links, or attempt to bypass safeguards. A publicly reachable page does not give permission to use its administrative functions.

Requests and decisions

Submitting a leave request does not mean it has been approved. Employees may cancel eligible pending requests immediately. For approved leave, an eligible cancellation request requires the boss’s decision. Until cancellation is approved, the original approved leave and its balance usage remain in effect.

The application implements company workflows; it does not replace employment agreements, company policies or applicable law. Ask HR to resolve disputed allowances, dates, sick leave entitlements or decisions. Usage notices are informational and are not medical advice or automatic disciplinary decisions.

Records and integrations

The HR database is the operational record for dashboard decisions. LINE notifications and Google Calendar entries are supporting views and can be delayed or fail. Editing a calendar event or a legacy spreadsheet does not automatically change the HR database. Report discrepancies to the administrator rather than assuming that one external view confirms a saved decision.

Calendar synchronization updates events managed by the system. Manually created events are handled separately. Reports reflect the selected period, employees, filters and the records available when generated.

Availability and corrections

The service depends on hosting and external providers and may experience interruptions. If an urgent request cannot be submitted or an approval is unclear, contact HR or your manager through the usual company channel. Administrators may correct records, with history retained where supported. Review important balances and rollover details before confirming changes.

Privacy

Read the Privacy Policy for the data handled, connected services, current access limitations and how to request corrections or deletion. Do not enter unnecessary sensitive information.

Changes and questions

Features and these terms may be updated as company processes change. Material changes should be communicated to affected users. Nothing here is intended to remove rights provided by applicable law or employment agreements.
