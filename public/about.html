TBS HR Leave

Last updated: 8 October 2026

An employee leave management system for TBS Marketing, connecting employee requests in LINE with company leave administration.

What the app does

  • Submit and review annual, sick and personal leave.
  • Track allowances, leave history and cancellation decisions.
  • Provide administrator reports and year rollover tools.
  • Synchronize approved leave to a shared Google Calendar and send LINE notifications.

Employees access the system through the company’s LINE account. Google Calendar is connected by the administrator for team scheduling.
