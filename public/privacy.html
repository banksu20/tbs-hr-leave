Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

TBS HR Leave is an employee leave management system used by TBS Marketing. This notice describes the information handled by the app and its connected services. Contact the system administrator at ratchadale26@gmail.com for privacy questions.

Information the system handles

The system processes employee names, nicknames, employee codes, departments, LINE account identifiers, leave types, dates, durations, reasons or notes, allowances, approvals, rejections, cancellation requests and change history. Sick leave records and any health information entered in notes may be sensitive. Avoid including medical details that are not needed for the request.

How information is used

Information is used to process leave and cancellation requests, calculate balances, produce reports, show team availability, send LINE status messages and sick leave usage notices, and maintain an audit trail. Technical logs help diagnose failed requests and integrations.

Google Calendar access

An administrator connects a Google account through OAuth to synchronize approved leave with the designated shared team calendar. The integration reads calendar access permissions and event identifiers, titles, dates and event metadata to compare events and avoid duplicates. It creates, updates or cancels events managed by TBS HR Leave; manually created events are preserved.

Managed event titles show employee identity and leave duration, with half-day details when available. Leave reasons and leave types are not included in these calendar events. Event visibility follows the shared calendar’s Google sharing settings. The integration does not send attendee invitations.

Google authorization tokens are held in n8n’s credential storage. Calendar responses and synchronization details may also be retained in workflow execution logs for troubleshooting.

Google API data is used for the calendar synchronization described here, not for advertising, sale, or training general-purpose AI models. Use and transfer of this data must comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy and the Google Workspace API User Data and Developer Policy, including their Limited Use requirements.

Services and access

The system uses Vercel to host the app, n8n to run workflows, PostgreSQL to store HR records, LINE for employee identity and messages, and Google Calendar for team scheduling. Some existing Google Sheets connections remain for registration and legacy lookups; automatic database-to-Sheets exports have been retired. These services process information needed for their respective functions.

Leave data is intended for employees and authorized company administrators. The CEO dashboard currently has no login restriction, so a person with its URL may be able to view records and perform actions. Do not treat that URL as an access control. Shared calendar members can see the information shown in calendar events.

Storage and retention

Leave records, decision history and related operational records remain in the system until removed through an administrative process. Cancelling leave does not necessarily delete its history. A fixed automatic retention or deletion period has not been configured for all records. Workflow logs, backups and copies in connected services may have different retention periods.

Your requests and choices

Contact ratchadale26@gmail.com or company HR to request access, correction or deletion of your information, or to raise a concern. Requests must be checked against applicable recordkeeping obligations and the records held in each connected service; immediate deletion of every copy cannot be promised.

The connected Google account owner can revoke the integration through Google account connections. Revocation stops future authorized calendar access but does not automatically delete existing calendar events or HR records. Contact the administrator if those records also need review or removal.

Changes

This notice will be updated when the system’s data handling changes. Material changes should be communicated to affected users.
